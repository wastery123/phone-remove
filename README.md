Allows you to control your phone from your computer. 
Simply connect the phone to your computer via USB, make sure to enable "USB debugging," and run `remove.exe`.
A simple menu window will appear with the following functions:
Turn the phone screen on/off
Open the camera
Take a screenshot (saved to a separate project folder on your PC)
Click mode (standard/long press)
Volume +/-
You can also send messages to your phone, and there are functions for "Back," "Home," and "Menu."
